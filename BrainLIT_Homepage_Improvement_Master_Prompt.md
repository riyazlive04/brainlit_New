# BrainLIT Homepage Improvement Master Prompt

# Objective

Redesign the existing BrainLIT homepage without changing its premium minimalist identity.

Keep approximately 80% of the current design language, typography, spacing philosophy, and branding. The goal is **not** to redesign from scratch but to improve conversion, trust, storytelling, and clarity while preserving the premium feel.

The homepage should feel comparable to Apple, Linear, Notion, and Stripe while maintaining a warm educational tone for parents.

---

# 1. Add a "Why Now?" Urgency Section

## Goal
Parents must understand why waiting is risky.

## Messaging

Headline:
> AI is changing childhood faster than schools.

Explain:
- AI writes homework.
- AI solves mathematics.
- AI writes essays.
- AI generates code.
- AI answers almost everything instantly.

The real question isn't whether children will use AI.

The question is whether they will know **how to think instead of depending on AI.**

Use statistics, timeline graphics, or AI adoption visuals.

# Final Instruction

Maintain BrainLIT's premium minimalist identity.

Do NOT clutter the interface.

Every new section should support one of four goals:

1. Build Trust
2. Increase Clarity
3. Improve Conversion

The final website should feel like a premium education brand rather than a conventional coaching institute.
