# BrainLIT Website Discovery Document

## 1. What does your business do?
BrainLIT is an AI-first thinking education company that helps children (ages 10–14) develop critical thinking, creativity, problem-solving, ethical AI usage, and entrepreneurship. We teach children how to think before they use AI, instead of simply teaching AI tools or coding.

## 2. Why are you building this website?
- Generate qualified parent leads.
- Convert visitors into webinar registrations and course enrollments.
- Build trust and authority.
- Showcase student projects, curriculum, testimonials, and podcast/content.
- Become the central digital platform for BrainLIT.

## 3. Who is your target audience?
Primary:
- Parents of children aged 10–14.
- English-speaking and Tamil-speaking parents in India (starting with Chennai).

Secondary:
- Schools.
- Educators.
- Students interested in AI and innovation.

## 4. What are your business goals?
- Become India's leading AI Thinking Academy for children.
- Consistently generate webinar registrations.
- Convert webinar attendees into paid students.
- Build a recognizable education brand.
- Scale to multiple online batches and future school partnerships.
